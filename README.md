# Groww MF FAQ Assistant

A facts-only FAQ chatbot for SBI Mutual Fund schemes available on Groww.

## Scope
- AMC: SBI Mutual Fund
- Schemes: SBI Bluechip Fund, SBI Flexicap Fund, SBI Long Term Equity Fund (ELSS)

## What it answers
- Expense ratios (TER)
- Exit loads
- Minimum SIP amounts
- ELSS lock-in period (3 years, Sec 80C)
- Riskometer ratings
- Benchmark indices
- Capital gains statement download

## What it refuses
Investment advice, fund comparisons, performance predictions.

## Sources
Public pages from SBI MF, AMFI, SEBI, Groww Support, MF Central.

## Disclaimer
Facts only. Not SEBI-registered financial advice. Always verify at official AMC/SEBI sources.
